Questions

AI and Machine Learning FAQs

Do you guarantee a specific accuracy?

No. Results depend on the quality and quantity of your data. We report the baseline, the final metrics and the cases where the model fails, so you can judge it on evidence.

How much data do we need?

It depends on the problem and the method. We assess this during scoping and tell you if the available data is enough to start.

Is the result a production system?

The first delivery is a working prototype: reproducible code, a baseline and an honest evaluation. Turning it into a production service is a separate scope.

Will we understand how the model works?

Yes. The handover includes the code, the data preparation steps, the metrics and a plain explanation of the model’s limits.
