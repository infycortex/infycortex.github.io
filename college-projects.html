Questions

College Project Development FAQs

Will you complete my project for me?

No. We build the working project and explain the code, architecture and presentation. The report, viva answers and submission must be your own work.

What do I need to provide to start?

Your topic or area of interest, your branch and any college format requirements, plus your deadline. If you do not have a topic yet, we will help you choose a feasible one.

How long does a college project take?

Usually 2–4 weeks depending on complexity, hardware availability and how quickly you can review progress.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Additional changes are quoted separately.

What do I receive at the end?

The working build, source code with an explanation, setup instructions, documentation and a guided session on how the modules fit together.

Academic Integrity

Built to Be Understood, Not Just Submitted

Our role is development support and explanation. Your submission stays yours.

What we do

  • Build and test the working project
  • Explain the architecture, database and code
  • Help you prepare for the presentation and viva

What we expect

  • You make sure you understand the work
  • The written report and submission are your own work
  • You follow your institution’s rules and deadlines
  • You raise questions early, before submission week

We will decline work that asks us to complete an academic submission on a student’s behalf.
