Working Together

General

How projects begin, and what you can expect from us at the start.

How does a project start?

Send the enquiry form, an email or a WhatsApp message with what you want to build. We reply with clarifying questions, then a written scope and quote. Work begins only after you confirm both.

What do I need to send to get started?

A short description of what you want built, who it is for, any deadline, and anything you already have — existing content, designs, data or a reference site. If you are unsure, send the idea and we will ask the questions that matter.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Projects run remotely through calls, email and file sharing, and calls are scheduled in advance when time zones differ. All quotes are in Indian rupees unless we agree otherwise.

Students

College Projects

What is included, and where our involvement stops.

Do you write the report or prepare viva answers?

No. We build the working project, explain the code and guide you through the finished project. The report, viva answers and submission must be your own work — that is the point of the project. Read the academic-integrity note on the College Projects page.

Will I understand my college project well enough for the viva?

That is how we build it. You get a guided explanation of the architecture, database and code, plus guidance on the questions a viva is likely to ask. You have to be able to explain the work yourself — we will not present it for you.

What does a college project package include?

A working build, source code with an explanation, setup instructions and a guided handover session. Scope and price are agreed in writing before we start, and the project stays yours to understand and present.
