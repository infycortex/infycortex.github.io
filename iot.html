Overview

What This Service Covers

We build connected systems from the sensor up: choose components, prototype on a breadboard, write and document the firmware, and present the data in a dashboard that works on real devices.

The hardware is documented as carefully as the code, so the system can be maintained, repaired and explained after handover.

Our Process

How an IoT Project Runs

Clear stages, agreed scope and a handover you can understand.

01

Requirement

We define what needs measuring, how often and what should happen when a value goes out of range.

02

Component selection

Boards and sensors are chosen for the job, with cost and availability discussed openly.

03

Breadboard prototype

The circuit is proven on a breadboard before any enclosure or permanent build.

04

Dashboard and alerts

Readings are displayed in a web dashboard and alerts follow the agreed channel.

05

Testing

Sensors, thresholds and connection loss are tested on the real setup.

06

Handover

Wiring diagram, documented firmware and guidance on maintenance.

Questions

IoT Solutions FAQs

Do you supply the hardware?

No. You purchase the components directly, and we provide the component list, wiring guidance and firmware support.

Which boards do you work with?

ESP32 for Wi-Fi-connected builds, and Arduino-compatible boards where simplicity and cost matter more.

Can I monitor the system from my phone?

The dashboard is a web interface that works in a normal mobile browser. Alerts are sent through the channel agreed during scoping.

Can you help with a college IoT project?

Yes. We build the working prototype and explain the wiring, firmware and data flow so you can present it yourself.
