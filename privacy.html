Privacy Policy

How InfyCortex Solution handles the information you share with us. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what happens to information you send through this website, email or WhatsApp. It applies to this site and to enquiries that begin here.

Information you give us

When you submit the enquiry form, you provide your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share there.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and answer your questions.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you proceed, deliver the work you asked for.
  • To keep a record of what was agreed.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not add you to unrelated marketing lists.

How it is stored

Form submissions are delivered through Formspree, a third-party form handling service, and are also received in our email inbox. Access is limited to the people who need it to respond and deliver the work.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and client information is kept for up to 12 months. It is deleted earlier if you ask us to.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the visitor’s IP address as part of serving those files.

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run this site and deliver the work — the form handling and email services described above — or where the law requires it. Links to third-party sites are covered by those sites’ own privacy policies.

Your choices

Email infycortex@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will respond within 7 working days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they apply to new enquiries.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to infycortex@gmail.com or through the contact page.

This policy is a plain-language draft prepared for InfyCortex Solution. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed before relying on it as a formal privacy statement.

Questions About Your Data?

Ask us directly and we will explain what we hold and how to have it removed.
