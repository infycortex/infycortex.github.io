Questions

Software Development FAQs

How is custom software priced?

Every project is quoted individually after we understand the scope. The quote is itemised, and we ask for 50% in advance with the balance before final handover.

Can you work with an existing system?

We review what you already have and advise whether integration, an upgrade or a replacement is most practical. We only recommend a rebuild when it is genuinely the better option.

Who owns the delivered code?

After final payment you receive the full source code and own the delivered work.

What support is included after delivery?

Every delivery includes 30 days of free bug-fix support. Ongoing maintenance and new features are quoted separately on request.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Any further changes are quoted separately.
