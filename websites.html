Questions

Website Development FAQs

Do you write the content for my website?

We can structure and edit the content you provide, and we will tell you where more detail is needed. We do not invent claims about your business — you approve the final copy before launch.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every layout is built to be responsive and checked from small phones up to large desktop screens before handover.

Can you update an existing website instead of rebuilding it?

Yes, where the existing platform allows it. We review the current site first and tell you honestly whether an update or a rebuild is the more practical choice.

Who owns the website and its code?

After final payment you receive the full source code and own the delivered work. We do not reuse your content or data publicly.

How do hosting and domains work?

We guide you through choosing and setting up hosting and a domain. Hosting and domain fees are paid by you directly to the provider.
